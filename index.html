Where do you pick up?

Our neighborhood around Chelsea. Book with your address and the shop will tell you if you're on a route.

How fast will I get it back?

Standard turnaround is 2 business days. Need it sooner or guaranteed? Say so when you book.

Is there a minimum?

Wash & fold has a 10 lb minimum ($20). Pickup and delivery are free for orders over $25.

What detergent do you use?

Plant-powered, hypoallergenic, biodegradable detergent, unscented by default. The 5 Trees scented option is $3/lb.

Can I make special requests?

Yes. We separate colors as standard. Add-ons include gentle care, extra rinse, vinegar rinse, hang dry and sanitize, plus hand wash, steam and ironing. For couture care, email reserve@ecolaundrycompany.com.
